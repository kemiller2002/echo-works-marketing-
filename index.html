Meet Jacquelyn Brice.

Jacquelyn is the founder and principal architect of EchoWorks. A graduate of Indiana University's J. Irwin Miller Architecture Program, she brings a considered approach to design, sustainability, human health, and the realities of construction.

Her credentials reflect that commitment: AIA, LEED BD+C, and WELL AP. EchoWorks is based in Indianapolis and Jacquelyn holds architecture licenses in Indiana and Ohio.
